Why teams use Spec2Tickets

⚡

Hours of work, gone

A page that used to take a day to break into tickets becomes a complete, structured backlog in minutes — Epic, stories, subtasks, acceptance criteria, sizing, and dependencies. No copy-paste, no hand-numbered checklists.

🎯

Your people, freed for real work

Analysts and product owners move from mechanical ticket-writing to refinement, prioritization, and stakeholder alignment. Developers start from full context — descriptions, acceptance criteria, and dependencies — instead of chasing ambiguity.

✓

Consistent quality, fewer gaps

Every breakdown meets the same bar — clear acceptance criteria, consistent structure, sizing, and dependency links. Fewer gaps, less rework, and cleaner handovers between analysts, developers, and QA.

Built for the whole delivery team

The mechanical part of turning a page into a backlog disappears — so everyone spends their time where it counts.

Business Analysts

Shift from transcribing requirements into tickets to creating value — refining, validating, and aligning with stakeholders.

Product Owners

Spend less time on backlog mechanics and more on what matters: priorities, scope, and outcomes.

Developers

Start coding from clear, complete work items — full context, acceptance criteria, and flagged risks — not a vague paragraph and a guess.

Your data, your key

Spec2Tickets is built privacy-first. You process your real Confluence pages without handing them to a third-party vendor.

Bring your own key

Your page content goes from Atlassian straight to your own Anthropic account, under your own agreement — billed to you, at cost.

No vendor backend

The app runs entirely on Atlassian Forge. Nothing is stored on our servers — because we don't operate any.

You stay in control

AI drafts; humans decide. You review and edit every breakdown, and nothing reaches Jira until you push it.
